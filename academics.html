Academics

A Clear Path from First Steps to Board Exams

CBSE-aligned curriculum, thoughtfully paced from Pre-Primary through Class 12.

Ages 3–5 · 2 Years

Pre-Primary

Our Pre-Primary program is entirely play-based, designed around how young children actually learn: through movement, story, and repetition. Class sizes are capped at 18 students with two dedicated adults in every room.

Subjects: Phonics & Early Literacy, Numbers & Pattern Recognition, Environmental Awareness, Fine Motor Skills.

Activities: Story Circle, Sensory Play, Music & Movement, Outdoor Exploration Hour.

Grades 1–5 · 5 Years

Primary

Primary years focus on building genuinely strong foundations in literacy and numeracy, without losing the joy of learning. Every classroom pairs direct instruction with hands-on project work each week.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Environmental Studies (EVS), Second Language (Hindi/French), Art & Craft, Physical Education.

Activities: Reading Clubs, Nature Study Walks, Craft Workshops, Class Assemblies, Inter-House Quizzes.

Grades 6–8 · 3 Years

Middle School

Subject specialisation begins here, with dedicated teachers for each discipline and a growing emphasis on project-based, collaborative learning.

Subjects: Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, Second Language, Computer Applications.

Activities: Annual Science Fair, Debate Club, Inter-House Sports, Model United Nations (introductory), Coding Club.

Grades 9–10 · 2 Years

High School

Board-aligned rigor paired with real career exploration — students start thinking seriously about their strengths and options well before Grade 11.

Subjects: Core CBSE curriculum (Math, Science, Social Science, English, Second Language) plus skill electives.

Activities: Career Fair, Model United Nations, Robotics Club, Inter-School Olympiads, Community Service Projects.

Grades 11–12 · 2 Years

Senior Secondary

Students choose from Science, Commerce, or Humanities streams, with dedicated mentoring for board exams and college applications.

Subjects: Stream-based CBSE curriculum with elective combinations tailored to career goals.

Activities: College Counselling Sessions, Summer Internships, National Olympiads, Peer Mentoring Program.
