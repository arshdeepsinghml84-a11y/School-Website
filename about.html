Our History

From One Classroom Block to a Full Campus

Riverstone Academy opened its doors in 1998 with three classrooms and forty students. Over the following decades we added Middle School, then Senior Secondary streams, science and computer labs, an auditorium, and eventually a second campus block.

Today we serve close to 1,850 students from Pre-Primary through Class 12, but the founding idea hasn't moved: small class sizes, teachers who stay for years, and a campus where every student is known by name.

What Guides Us

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Mission

To nurture every learner's intellectual curiosity, character, and confidence through a balanced academic and co-curricular experience.

Vision

To be a school where students leave not just qualified for the next stage of life, but genuinely ready for it.

Integrity & Respect

Honesty and respect for others sit at the center of our discipline policy and daily classroom culture.

Curiosity

We build lessons around real questions, not just answers to memorise for a test.

Resilience

We teach students to sit with a hard problem rather than give up on the first try.

Community

Families, teachers, and alumni stay connected long after graduation day.

Leadership

A Message from Our Principal

"We measure our success not by the marks on a report card, but by the kind of adults our students become. Every policy on this campus, from our discipline approach to our extracurricular calendar, is built around that one idea."

Dr. Anjali Sharma, Principal, M.Ed., Ph.D. in Education

Dr. Sharma has led Riverstone Academy since 2015, following twelve years as Vice Principal. She holds a doctorate in Education Leadership and continues to teach a Grade 12 seminar each year.
