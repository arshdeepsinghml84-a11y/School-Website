How It Works

Four Simple Steps to Join Riverstone

  1. 1

    Online Form

    Fill out the enquiry and application form on our admissions portal. Takes about 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    Document Upload

    Submit birth certificate, previous school records, address proof, and passport photos.

  3. 3

    Interview

    A short, friendly conversation with the student and parents — no formal testing below Grade 6.

  4. 4

    Admission Confirmation

    Receive your offer letter and complete fee registration to secure the seat.

Before You Apply

Eligibility by Grade

Pre-Primary (Nursery)

Age 3 by 31 March

No entrance test. A short, playful interaction session with the child and one parent.

Grades 1–5

Age-appropriate

No entrance test. Previous school report reviewed where applicable.

Grades 6–10

Age-appropriate

Written assessment in English and Math, followed by a parent-student interview.

Grades 11–12

Stream-dependent

Minimum qualifying marks in Grade 10 boards; stream seats allotted subject to availability.
