Campus Facilities

Built for Learning, Play, and Discovery

Every facility on campus is designed around one question: does this help a child grow?

Take a Closer Look

Explore Our Campus

Science Labs

Separate physics, chemistry, and biology labs with individual workstations for hands-on experiments from Grade 6 onward.

Computer Labs

Two computer labs with one device per student, covering coding, robotics, and digital literacy from Grade 3.

Library

28,000+ titles across fiction, reference, and periodicals, with a dedicated quiet reading wing open before and after school.

Auditorium

A 600-seat auditorium hosting assemblies, Annual Day, and inter-school competitions throughout the year.

Sports Complex

Football pitch, basketball and volleyball courts, a running track, and indoor facilities for badminton and table tennis.

Swimming Pool

A supervised, temperature-controlled pool with certified instructors for beginner through competitive swim training.

Cafeteria

A nutritionist-reviewed menu, allergy-aware seating, and a dedicated space for packed lunches.

Medical Room

A full-time nurse on campus during school hours, with a doctor on call for emergencies.

Transport

GPS-tracked buses with attendants on every route, covering most neighbourhoods within 15km of campus.

Smart Classes

Interactive panels and digital content in every classroom from Grade 3 upward.
