About Riverstone Academy

Rooted in Values, Growing Toward the Future

Founded in 1998, Riverstone Academy has grown from a single block of classrooms into a full Pre-Primary-to-Class-12 campus. What hasn't changed is our belief that children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and genuinely challenged.

Our Mission

To nurture every learner's intellectual curiosity, character, and confidence through a balanced academic and co-curricular experience.

Our Vision

To be a school where students leave not just qualified for the next stage of life, but genuinely ready for it.

Core Values

Integrity, Curiosity, Respect, Resilience, and Community guide every decision we make, in the classroom and beyond it.

"We measure our success not by the marks on a report card, but by the kind of adults our students become."

Dr. Anjali Sharma, Principal
